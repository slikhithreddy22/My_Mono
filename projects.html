01

Mnemo / Engram

active

A privacy-preserving, CPU-only episodic memory retrieval system for egocentric video — ask it what happened and when, without a GPU or a cloud upload. This is my final-year research project, and the core academic focus right now. The pipeline runs YOLOv8 and MediaPipe for detection, Farneback optical flow for motion, CLIP for semantics, and a small ~5K-parameter MLP to fuse it all, indexed through ChromaDB and SQLite, queried via phi3-mini or Moondream2 over Ollama. Video ingestion (milestone 1) is done; next up is live RTSP, Whisper voice queries, and a web/mobile UI. Targeting ACM Multimedia, IEEE TENCON, or BMVC 2026 for publication.

Setu

early dev

An independent Indian Sign Language translation app. Pose sequences captured through MediaPipe are decoded into text through a T5 model, trained on the iSign dataset (~228GB, currently staged through Google Colab with Drive persistence). Still early — scaffolding and data pipeline stage.
